Three real recognition errors involved an extra rest at the second system, shifting later notes. The adapter now audits measure duration and rereads suspicious images at +2° / -2°, choosing among actual image-derived results. It never deletes a note/rest or forces durations to fit. Remaining inconsistencies and detected/recognized note-count disagreement reach the app as review warnings. Predicted lower-staff notes and unresolved multi-staff layouts are rejected rather than discarded.

**Qualification limits:** these are 32 original generated compositions with controlled variants, not 148 distinct camera photographs. There are zero actual camera photographs, zero independently checked references and zero qualified handwritten guitar pages. Therefore the accepted 100-image release qualification and real-camera target remain unmet despite 100% note-event F1 on this controlled set.

That is neither guitar qualification nor sufficient evidence to advertise handwriting. The research-only dataset has noncommercial terms, stays in ignored artifacts, and is not shipped, redistributed with the app or used to train a production model. Zeus is not a production dependency.
