# Familiar Classics: composition and source review

Reviewed 7 September 2026. This is a documented source-selection assessment, not a promise that every country's law is identical. The release uses instrumental melodies from identifiable historical editions and new StringMap guitar voicings. It contains no source scans, lyrics, historical accompaniment parts, external recordings, commercial tabs, or modern guitar arrangements.

## Territorial basis

US status is considered separately from international status. The [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) identifies works published before 1931 as outside their US copyright term. For known composers below, even a life-plus-100-year calculation has elapsed; that conservative comparison is relevant to [Mexico's Article 29](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf_mov/Ley_Federal_del_Derecho_de_Autor.pdf), rather than assuming the US publication rule applies everywhere. The [UK Act, section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/12) uses life plus 70 years and separate provisions for unknown authors. Anonymous historical melodies below were publicly documented over 135 years ago, with substantially earlier origins where identified. These findings support broad availability under ordinary composition copyright terms. They do not authorize copying later editorial contributions or recordings, nor make a blanket worldwide-clearance representation.

The [Copyright Office's music guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/musicians/) distinguishes a musical composition from its recording. Playback uses the separately licensed, already bundled CC0 guitar SoundFont. The app makes its own synthesized performances.

## Replacements and boundaries

The Water Is Wide and When the Saints Go Marching In were not used: their initially selected versions were insufficiently pinned to an inspected historical melody. The approved replacement order supplied Flow Gently, Sweet Afton and Drink to Me Only with Thine Eyes. La Bamba and Au Clair de la Lune were unnecessary. No licensing purchase is required for the selected material under this assessment.

The score-reference check is not a substitute for harmony, playback, UI, or physical-audio qualification. Those checks are reported separately. Do not describe historical variants as a transcription of a contemporary recording or advertise these as modern popular hits.
