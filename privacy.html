StringMap privacy policy

Updated September 5, 2026. StringMap on iPhone and iPad.

Your library stays on your device

Imported MusicXML, corrected scores, saved source images, guitar settings, pinned positions, and practice progress are stored in the app on your device. StringMap does not operate a cloud library. Saved music and playback work offline. Device backups may include this data under your Apple backup settings.

Scanning a page

You choose or photograph a page, crop it, and approve sending that image. Upload begins only when you tap Recognize Notes or Resume recognition. Scanning requires internet. The recognition service receives the prepared image and returns detected notes as MusicXML. We do not use your pages for advertising, analytics, or model training.

Uploaded images and intermediate files are deleted after recognition completes, fails, or is cancelled. A service restart removes abandoned working files. Unclaimed recognition results and job records expire within one hour after processing ends. When your app receives a result, it requests deletion immediately. If a network failure prevents deletion, the expiry still applies.

An unfinished scan is saved privately on your device so you can resume it. It is removed after saving or cancelling, or when you reopen scanning after seven days. Once you save a corrected score, its source image stays with that library item until you remove it or delete the app.

Anonymous security and operational data

There are no customer accounts, advertising identifiers, tracking SDKs, or analytics. Apple's App Attest verifies that scan requests come from StringMap. The service stores an app-specific public key, a signed verification receipt, and an assertion counter. These security records expire after 90 days without use. Anonymous session tokens expire after one hour.

The server sees your IP address when you connect. Application rate limits retain a hash of the address for up to one hour. Scan limits use the app-specific key and expire within one day. Operational messages contain status, processing time, and resource information, never image contents or score text. The hosting provider processes network traffic to deliver and secure the service.

Camera and Photos

Camera permission is requested only when you choose Take a Photo. The system Photos picker shares only the images you select with StringMap; it does not grant the app unrestricted access to your photo library. You can decline camera access and still import MusicXML or choose an existing photo.

Local playback

The bundled alphaTab renderer and SoundFont run on your device through an isolated web view and a private loopback connection. This connection does not upload music. Development builds can connect to a developer-configured recognition endpoint; release builds use the hosted service.

Public support website

The public support and policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records website visitors’ IP addresses for security under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . The website contains no analytics scripts or advertising.

Your choices

You can cancel scanning, remove individual library items, or delete the app to remove its local data. App removal does not remove earlier device backups. Service data follows the automatic retention periods above. StringMap does not sell personal data or track you across apps or websites.

Contact

Privacy and support: stringmap.support@gmail.com. Please avoid emailing sensitive images unless they are needed to resolve your request.
