# Original StringMap exercises

All 18 pieces were newly composed for StringMap in this development session. They are short pedagogical pitch/rhythm sequences, encoded in `scripts/build-release-exercises.py`; no external song, MusicXML, PDF, MuseScore arrangement, or test-pack score was copied or adapted.

The authored manifest specifies sounding MIDI pitch, onset, duration, measure and identity independently of the Swift importer and alphaTab renderer. MusicXML uses conventional guitar written pitch with an explicit octave-down transposition. Each four-bar phrase is written out twice, making eight bars without repeat-navigation ambiguity.

This is provenance, not a claim that automated checks constitute human listening. Technical verification and visual/device review status are recorded in the release evidence report.
