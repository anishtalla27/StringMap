# StringMap support

## Start an exercise

Open Learn → Free Practice and choose one of 18 original single-note guitar exercises. Standard notation and tablature appear in Play. The fretboard shows the current note's string and fret, with the next position indicated separately. String 1 is the high E string; string 6 is the low E string. Fret 0 means play the open string.

The included music uses standard tuning, E–A–D–G–B–E from lowest to highest string. Guitar notation is written one octave above its sounding pitch. The fretboard and audio use the sounding pitch.

## Tutorial Mode

Open Learn → Tutorial Mode, or Continue Tutorial on Home. Every lesson is accessible. Follow See it, Hear it, Try it, and Recap at your own pace. Completion records participation, not a test of mastery. Your last lesson, step, example, tempo, and position are saved on this device.

The teaching fretboard focuses on frets 0–5. Marker numbers indicate fretting fingers; fret numbers appear below the board. A hollow circle means an open string, and an X means do not play that string. Tap an included position to hear its note during the explanation. During Try it, tapping answers the Find this note question. The fret choices below the question provide an accessible alternative.

Show tab adds tablature below standard notation. Left-handed reverses the fretboard orientation without changing string numbers or pitches. Previous and Next move one musical step; Hear this step auditions a note or chord. Tutorial Mode uses standard tuning with no capo. Changing lessons leaves your Free Practice song and settings intact. The public guided course does not listen to your microphone or grade your playing.

## Playback and practice

Tap Play to start, then Pause to hold your place. Tap Restart to clear any loop and return to the beginning. Drag Playback position to seek. Open Practice to change BPM, choose a speed preset, navigate measures, loop a passage, or enable count-in and metronome. Tempo changes keep pitch intact. The BPM range is one quarter to twice the exercise's original tempo.

An exercise is saved locally when you first open it. Opening it again restores its saved practice settings. Your library stays available offline. You can remove your saved copy from Library and start fresh from Learn → Free Practice.

## Fretboard and instrument

The highlighted marker and the note/string/fret readout show where to play the current note. A rest has no sounding position. Tap the fretboard to choose another valid fingering, or expand it for a larger view. Instrument settings include tuning, capo and transposition; changing them may make a passage unplayable, in which case StringMap explains the problem. Restore standard tuning, no capo, and zero transposition to use the original exercise.

## Scope of this release

StringMap includes a 24-lesson course from beginner foundations to early-intermediate playing and 18 bundled single-note exercises. Tutorial Mode includes authored open chords, small major/minor triads, fingerpicking, and introductory hammer-ons and pull-offs. Photo scanning, handwriting, general chord import, and public file import are not included. There is no account, purchase, or recognition service to configure.

## If playback is silent

Check device volume and the selected output. After a call or disconnecting headphones, return to the app and tap Play. If notation or playback fails to load, reopen the exercise and then restart the app if needed.
