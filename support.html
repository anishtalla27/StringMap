StringMap support

Guitar notation, tablature, and practice

Import a single-part guitar MusicXML score (.musicxml or .xml) or compressed MusicXML file (.mxl), choose a fingering profile, and play the notation alongside tablature and the fretboard. Stacked notes and independent guitar voices are preserved. Tuning, capo, transposition, loops, tempo, metronome, count-in, seeking, and pinned positions are saved with your music.

Scan and correct a page

Open Import, choose Scan guitar notation, then take a photo or choose one from Photos. Crop, rotate, and straighten the page. Keep the clef and key signature visible. Approve the image upload and tap Recognize Notes. Scanning needs internet and can take several minutes.

Compare every detected note and rhythm with the source image in Review Notes. Tap a note to edit its pitch, accidental, octave, duration, onset, voice, or ties. Add or remove notes and rests, and use the same onset for stacked notes. Use Measure length to preserve or correct silence at the end of a measure; note and rest durations are never shortened by this control. Update notation and playback to audition corrections. Check the review confirmation before saving.

To correct a saved scan, press and hold its Library card and choose Review source and correct notes. Your practice settings stay with the song. Corrections and recent undo history are saved as you work. Close the review or reopen the app to resume them. Use Discard unfinished edits to restore the starting version without deleting the photo or song. A saved song keeps its previous playable version until you confirm and save the review. Resume an unfinished new page from the scan screen; once recognition has finished, reviewing it does not need internet.

Supported notation and limitations

StringMap supports one guitar part on one staff, stacked chords, independent voices, rests, ties, binary rhythms, triplets, and simple repeats. Piano grand staffs, chord-name interpretation such as G minor, grace notes, other tuplets, repeat endings, and expressive marks that change playback are not supported. MIDI, PDF, and existing tablature are not accepted as MusicXML imports.

Recognition accuracy, especially handwriting, is still under evaluation. Handwriting is not an advertised supported feature in this version. Never assume recognition is correct without comparing it to the page.

Octaves and unplayable passages

MusicXML instrument transposition is applied once. For photos, Guitar notation is the default: written E4 sounds as E3 for tab and playback. Choose Concert pitch before reviewing a page written at sounding pitch. Review note names show sounding pitches; use the review octave controls to correct them if needed. An explicit MusicXML transposition takes precedence, so the octave is not applied twice. StringMap never changes octaves just to fit notes on the guitar.

If the notes cannot be played together on six strings with the selected tuning and reach, the app flags the passage and retains notation and playback for correction. Changing instrument settings may resolve it. The source notes are not discarded.

Playback and offline use

Saved scores, MusicXML import, fingering, notation, and playback work offline. Check volume and the selected audio output if playback is silent. After an interruption or disconnecting headphones, return to the app and tap Play.
