StringMap support

Guitar notation, tablature, and practice

Import a single-part guitar MusicXML score, choose a fingering profile, and play the notation alongside tablature and the fretboard. Stacked notes and independent guitar voices are preserved. Tuning, capo, transposition, loops, tempo, metronome, count-in, seeking, and pinned positions are saved with your music.

Scan and correct a page

Open Import, choose Scan guitar notation, then take a photo or choose one from Photos. Crop, rotate, and straighten the page. Keep the clef and key signature visible. Approve the image upload and tap Recognize Notes. Scanning needs internet and can take several minutes.

Compare every detected note and rhythm with the source image in Review Notes. Tap a note to edit its pitch, accidental, octave, duration, onset, voice, or ties. Add or remove notes and rests, and use the same onset for stacked notes. Update notation and playback to audition corrections. Check the review confirmation before saving.

To correct a saved scan, press and hold its Library card and choose Review source and correct notes. Your practice settings stay with the song. An unfinished page can be resumed from the scan screen after an interruption.

Supported notation and limitations

StringMap supports one guitar part on one staff, stacked chords, independent voices, rests, ties, binary rhythms, triplets, and simple repeats. Piano grand staffs, chord-name interpretation such as G minor, grace notes, other tuplets, repeat endings, and expressive marks that change playback are not supported. Compressed MXL, MIDI, PDF, and existing tablature are not accepted as MusicXML imports.

Recognition accuracy, especially handwriting, is still under evaluation. Handwriting is not an advertised supported feature in this version. Never assume recognition is correct without comparing it to the page.

Octaves and unplayable passages

MusicXML instrument transposition is applied once. Guitar commonly sounds an octave below written notation; an image recognizer may omit this information. Listen and compare, then use the review octave controls if needed. StringMap never changes octaves just to fit notes on the guitar.

If the notes cannot be played together on six strings with the selected tuning and reach, the app flags the passage and retains notation and playback for correction. Changing instrument settings may resolve it. The source notes are not discarded.

Playback and offline use

Saved scores, MusicXML import, fingering, notation, and playback work offline. Check volume and the selected audio output if playback is silent. After an interruption or disconnecting headphones, return to the app and tap Play.

Privacy and help

Read Privacy policy in Settings for upload handling and retention. Contact stringmap.support@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and the app version/build from Settings. Please include a score only when you have permission to share it.
